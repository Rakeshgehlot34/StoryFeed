# ShortsSplitter Privacy Policy & Data Governance

A transparent, plain-language explanation of how ShortsSplitter handles your media files, data storage, and device permissions.

---

## 1. 100% On-Device Local Processing

ShortsSplitter is built on a strict privacy-first foundation:
- **No Cloud Uploads:** Your videos, audio tracks, and exported clips never leave your device. All video splitting, transcoding, and filtering are performed entirely offline using local binaries.
- **No Internet Access:** ShortsSplitter does not request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission in its Android manifest. The application cannot transmit any data over Wi-Fi or cellular networks.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** ShortsSplitter contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising trackers, and zero behavioral telemetry tools.

---

## 2. What Data Is Stored Locally?

All data generated by ShortsSplitter is stored strictly on your local device storage:

1. **User Preferences:**
   - Preferred theme mode (Light, Dark, System) and creator accent color.
   - Default clip duration preset and preferred duplicate file handling strategy.
   - Saved in local `SharedPreferences`.
2. **Project History Metadata:**
   - File name of processed videos, duration, total clips created, timestamp, and saved file paths.
   - Saved in local `SharedPreferences` to populate your personal Dashboard and History screens.
3. **Temporary Files & Thumbnails:**
   - Intermediate video segments and generated overlay badge images stored temporarily in the app-private cache directory (`/data/user/0/.../cache/`).
   - Automatically purged upon export completion.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

ShortsSplitter requests only the minimum permissions necessary to process and save your clips:

| Permission | OS Version | Why ShortsSplitter Needs It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Videos / Storage** (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) | Android 9+ | Required to allow you to select a video file from your device storage to split. |
| **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | Android 13+ | Required to display an ongoing export progress bar in your notification tray when you switch apps. |
| **Foreground Service** (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING`) | Android 14+ | Prevents Android battery optimization from pausing or killing lengthy video transcoding jobs. |
| **Wake Lock** (`WAKE_LOCK`) | Android 9+ | Prevents the device CPU from sleeping while FFmpeg encodes video frames. |
| **Write Storage** (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) | Android 9 only | Required solely on Android 9 (API 28) to write finished clips to your public `Movies` folder. Never requested on Android 10+. |

---

## 4. How to Clear Stored Data & Cache

You maintain complete control over your data:

1. **Clear Temporary Cache:**
   - Open **Settings** within ShortsSplitter.
   - Scroll to **Processing & Performance**.
   - Tap **Clear Temporary Files & Thumbnails** to erase all cached files.
2. **Delete Project History:**
   - Open the **History** tab.
   - Swipe any project card left or tap the trash icon to remove it.
   - Long-press to select and delete multiple projects simultaneously.
3. **Reset All Application State:**
   - Open **Settings** > scroll to **About** > tap **Reset All Settings & History**.
   - Alternatively, open Android **Settings** > **Apps** > **ShortsSplitter** > **Storage** > tap **Clear Data**.
